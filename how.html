How SHEMS Works

Smart Home Energy Management System

An ATS (Automatic Transfer Switch) is a simple device with one job: when grid power fails, it switches the home to a battery or generator. It does not know how much power anything uses, it cannot be controlled remotely and it sends no alerts.

SHEMS uses the same "switch on outage" idea, but only as one small part of a bigger system. The project is the monitoring, control and alert layer built on top of that switch.

The 4 functions of SHEMS

  1. Monitoring – measures the current and power of each critical load on its own, in real time (not just on/off).
  2. Remote control – turn any load on or off from this app or by an SMS command, from anywhere.
  3. Alerting – sends an instant SMS the moment power is lost, even if you are not home.
  4. Load management – tracks the total consumption against the battery's 60 W capacity to prevent overload.
